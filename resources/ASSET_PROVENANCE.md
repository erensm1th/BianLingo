# BianLingo artwork

The two-cat logo is original artwork provided for BianLingo. The gray cat, cream cat, white and cream regions, and cream paws are all part of the artwork.

`branding/logo.png` is the original PNG. `bianlingo.ico` and `addon/BianLingo/Textures/Logo.tga` are resized and format-converted copies of the complete two-cat composition, with only empty exterior white margins trimmed for clearer small icons. `tools/generate_project_icon.py` preserves the original PNG, cream areas and paws; it does not redraw the artwork. File hashes are recorded in `branding/manifest.json`.

The logo uses no Blizzard artwork or game textures. See [Artwork](branding/README.md) for asset handling.

`branding/duanduan.png` is an original transparent pixel portrait supplied by the project author. Its ICO and TGA derivatives preserve the supplied colors and alpha; their hashes and the source hash are recorded in the artwork manifest.

`branding/bianbian.png` is the original gray-cat pixel portrait supplied for the project. `resources/bianbian.ico` preserves its colors and transparency at multiple display sizes. Source and derivative hashes are included in the artwork manifest.
