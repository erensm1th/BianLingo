# BianLingo artwork

I drew the two-cat logo for BianLingo. The gray cat, cream cat, white and cream regions, and cream paws are all part of the artwork.

`branding/logo.png` is the original PNG. `bianlingo.ico` and `addon/BianLingo/Textures/Logo.tga` are resized and format-converted copies of the complete two-cat composition, with only empty exterior white margins trimmed for clearer small icons. `tools/generate_project_icon.py` preserves the original PNG, cream areas and paws; it does not redraw the artwork. File hashes are recorded in `branding/manifest.json`.

The logo uses no Blizzard artwork or game textures. See [Artwork](branding/README.md) for asset handling.
